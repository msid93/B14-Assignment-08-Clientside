# Studio photography

The six fictional interiors in `public/photos/` were made for StudioSpace with OpenAI image generation. They depict sample spaces for the catalogue. The images are included locally so the home page and the seed listings do not depend on a remote photo service.

| File | Scene |
| --- | --- |
| `daylight.webp` | Golden-hour photography loft with factory windows and a white cyclorama |
| `film.webp` | Industrial soundstage with a blue sweep, lighting rig and director’s chair |
| `podcast.webp` | Walnut-and-green conversation room with two chairs and microphones |
| `music.webp` | Recording suite with an analogue desk and a view into the live room |
| `workshop.webp` | Daylit printmaking and ceramics room with a long shared bench |
| `portrait.webp` | Terracotta portrait set with a bentwood chair and studio softbox |

The six `-detail.webp` images are second camera angles of the same six generated rooms. Every room has a wide shot and a closer view for its gallery. Photographs are stored in WebP format. The source direction was an empty, shoot-ready room photographed for a location catalogue, using a wide architectural lens, natural material texture and practical lighting. Each listing’s gallery can be extended with the host’s own photographs.

## Campaign cover

`portrait-campaign.webp` is an original OpenAI-generated fashion editorial for the Terracotta Corner homepage slide. The studio cover supplied the location reference. A fictional model in ivory tailoring stands on the terracotta sweep, seen from behind the photographer.

## Typography

Manrope and Newsreader Italic are self-hosted Latin variable fonts. Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Font files and licence notices are in public/fonts/.
